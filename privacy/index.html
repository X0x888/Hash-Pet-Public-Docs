Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-07

Short version: HashPet processes browsing root domains (like example.com) only when Domain Capture is ON, stores data locally in your browser, and does not transmit your browsing data to a server.

1) What HashPet is

HashPet is a Chrome extension that turns your browsing into a virtual pet experience. HashPet does not require an account and is designed to run on-device.

2) Data HashPet accesses

Browsing domains (root domains only)

  • What: The root domain of pages you visit (e.g. news.ycombinator.com → ycombinator.com).
  • When: Only when Domain Capture is enabled in HashPet settings.
  • What it does not read: Page path/query strings, page content, form data, or keystrokes.

3) Data HashPet stores

HashPet stores data locally in your browser via chrome.storage.local, including:

  • Pet/game state (stats, evolution state, digestion queue, waste level, etc.).
  • Captured domains (root domains) required for gameplay features.
  • Preferences (Domain Capture toggle, notifications toggle, auto-digest toggle, etc.).

4) Data HashPet does not collect

  • HashPet does not transmit browsing domains or pet data to any developer-controlled server.
  • HashPet does not use the Chrome History API to read your history.
  • HashPet does not collect page contents, passwords, cookies, or messages.

5) Optional notifications

Notifications are optional and off by default. If you enable notifications, HashPet requests the notifications permission at that time.

6) Feedback (Google Forms)

If you choose to send feedback, you may be redirected to a Google Form. Submissions to the Google Form are handled by Google under their own policies, and may include any text you provide. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information.

Open Feedback Form

7) Your choices and controls

  • Turn Domain Capture ON/OFF in HashPet settings.
  • Delete all stored data via HashPet settings → “Delete All Data”.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes its local storage.

8) Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the effective date at the top of this page when we do.

9) Contact

For questions or requests, use the feedback form.
